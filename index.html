How we run a site

Four habits that show up on every job, whatever the size.

One report, every Friday

Programme against plan, cost against budget, photographs from each area and the three biggest open risks. Sent to you, not saved for the next meeting.

A price that matches the drawings

Lump sum once the drawings are complete. Open-book with a capped fee while the design is still moving. You choose at tender, and we explain what each choice costs you.

Safe before fast

Every worker is inducted before entering. A toolbox talk opens each shift. An independent safety audit runs monthly and the result goes on the site board.

Handover with nothing missing

A defects walk-round with you before the keys change hands. Snags closed within 14 days. As-built drawings, manuals and warranties delivered in one indexed folder.
